Biometric & Web Clock-In

Configure allowed clock-in methods — biometric devices, web-based punching, and device-level controls.

Clock-In Methods
Enable Biometric Device Punch

Allow employees to clock in/out via registered fingerprint or face recognition devices.

Enable Web Clock-In

Allow employees to punch in/out from the browser-based portal.

Require Selfie Capture on Punch

Mandatory selfie photo at each clock-in event for verification purposes.

Restrict to Whitelisted Devices

Only allow clock-in from pre-approved devices registered in the system.

Timing & Attempt Controls
Minutes after shift start before late-mark applies
Maximum failed attempts before lockout
Send reminder N minutes before shift end
Enable Clock-Out Reminder Notifications
