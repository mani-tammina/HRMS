Help Resources

Quick Guidance

To override statutory deductions (PF, ESI, PT, TDS), navigate to Finance > Tax Admin or edit the employee's payroll assignment overrides directly.

Once payroll is locked, payslips can be generated and published to employees via Manage Payslips under the Payroll Outcome section.

Download your bank's NEFT/RTGS bulk upload spreadsheet by clicking View Pay Register and selecting your designated bank format.

Off-cycle runs can be executed at any point through the Run Stepper by selecting the off-cycle run type option.

Detailed wage formulas, gross-to-net calculations, and statutory schedules can be audited under Review all employees before finalizing payroll.
