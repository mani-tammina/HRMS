Payroll Support & Resources

Frequently asked questions about Leave, Attendance & Payable Units:

  • How are LOP days calculated? Unrecorded attendance without an approved leave or regularization defaults to LOP.
  • Why validate leave requests first? Approved leaves count as paid days and prevent unwarranted deductions during Step 2 & 3.
  • Can I override LOP manually? Yes, you can manually adjust payable units during Step 3 or mark specific days as Present in Step 2.
  • When are prior month regularizations refunded? Verified regularizations appear in Step 4: LOP Reversal.
