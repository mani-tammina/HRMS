Hold & Arrears Guidelines

Quick compliance tips for handling withheld salaries and retroactive adjustments.

1. Administrative Hold

Holding a salary pauses payout generation for the affected employee while keeping all other employees in the run active. Bank transfers will skip held employees.

2. Arrears & Statutory Taxes

Arrears added to this month are treated as taxable gross earnings. When payroll executes, TDS and EPF calculations automatically incorporate these sums.

3. Releasing Withheld Pay

When unholding an employee, toggle "Pay as Arrear" to disburse their previously held wages directly in the current payroll batch.
