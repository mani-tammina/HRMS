Help Resources ∧
  • How is prorated salary calculated for new joinees? ↗

    Prorated salary is calculated as (Monthly Gross ÷ Total Calendar Days in Month) × Units Worked from Date of Joining to month-end.

  • When should an employee's salary be held? ↗

    Hold salary when bank details are unverified, onboarding documentation is pending, or when an exiting employee requires F&F settlement clearances.

  • How does notice shortfall recovery work? ↗

    Any shortfall in mandatory notice days is deducted as per the daily gross rate during Full & Final settlement calculation.

  • Can new joinees be excluded from this month's payroll? ↗

    Yes, select "Hold salary" or "Do not process" in the Pay Action dropdown to defer payment to subsequent payroll cycles.
