Help Resources ∧
  • How is the hourly overtime rate calculated? ↗

    Statutory OT rate is calculated as: Monthly Gross Salary ÷ (26 working days × 8 hours per day). Normal overtime applies at 1.5x, and holiday/weekend overtime at 2.0x.

  • How are revision arrears computed? ↗

    Arrears equal the difference between revised monthly gross and previous monthly gross multiplied by the number of retroactively applicable months.

  • Is performance bonus subject to TDS? ↗

    Yes, performance bonuses and ex-gratia are treated as taxable salary additions in the payout month and are subjected to normal income tax deduction.

  • Can bonus or overtime payouts be held? ↗

    Yes, select "Hold bonus" or "Hold OT" in the Pay Action dropdown to defer disbursement to subsequent pay cycles.
